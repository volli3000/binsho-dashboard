Method

Return is time-weighted (TWR), after trading commissions and before taxes: deposits and withdrawals are removed, so the figure reflects the strategy, not the timing of capital. Drawdown is measured on that return index. Drawdown ratio = Binsho's maximum drawdown divided by the benchmark's over the same period; below 1 means shallower losses. It is shown once the benchmark has fallen at least 1 %. CAGR and Calmar ratio (CAGR ÷ max drawdown) are shown after 12 months.

Out-of-sample only. The record starts on the first day of operation. No backtest data is shown or blended in. Benchmarks are fully invested buy-and-hold positions from the same start date.
